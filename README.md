A phone's last words as its battery hits 1 percent — in its own voice, one
concrete detail, one twist, no will, no charger goodbye, no 1-percent pun.

> I've kept your ex's number blocked for 214 days. The block dies with me. Good luck.

`main.ts` writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (83 characters,
inside the 1–199 gate). Entry for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-agentfid001-famous-last-words-at-1-percent-63e2b0b4).
